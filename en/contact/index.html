Frequently asked questions

Is Go Tirana free to use?

Yes. The guide and the bus planner are free, and you don't need an account.

How accurate are the bus times?

Routes come from OpenStreetMap and journey times are estimates based on how often each line runs. There are no live arrival times yet, so allow a little extra time.

Can I suggest a place or report a mistake?

Yes, please. Use the form on this page and choose “Suggest a place” or “Report a mistake”.

Is Go Tirana run by the Municipality of Tirana?

No. It is an independent guide built on open data and is not affiliated with the Municipality.
