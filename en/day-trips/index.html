Beyond the city

Day trips from Tirana

Four towns you can reach by bus in a day: when the buses run, what they cost and what to see when you get there.

All buses leave from one terminal

Buses to these towns leave from the North & South Bus Terminal, outside the centre near the Casa Italia shopping centre. A city bus takes you there for 40 lek, or a taxi costs around €10–15.

Pay the driver in cash. Times change with the season, so ask at the terminal for the last bus back.

Skanderbeg's mountain fortress

Krujë

A hilltop town with the castle where Skanderbeg held off the Ottomans, a museum to him inside the walls and a cobbled bazaar full of crafts.

Travel time
About 1 hour
Ticket
About 200 lek
Buses
About every hour, 07:00–18:00

What to see

  • Krujë CastleWalk the walls for views down to the coast on a clear day.
  • Skanderbeg MuseumThe story of the 15th-century hero, inside the castle.
  • Old bazaarFelt hats, carpets, copper and antiques along a cobbled street.

When you arrive: The bus stops below the old town; the bazaar and castle are a short uphill walk.

Our tip: Half a day is enough. Combine it with lunch in the bazaar and you are back in Tirana by late afternoon.

Roman ruins and the sea

Durrës

Albania's oldest city and main port: a Roman amphitheatre in the middle of town, a seafront promenade and the closest beach to Tirana.

Travel time
40–45 minutes
Ticket
About 200 lek
Buses
Every 15–30 minutes, 06:00–21:00

What to see

  • Roman amphitheatreOne of the largest in the Balkans, with an early Christian chapel inside.
  • Venetian Tower and wallsA round medieval tower on the old city walls, now a café.
  • Seafront promenadeWalk along the bay at sunset and finish with fresh fish.

When you arrive: Buses arrive at the Durrës bus station next to the port, a 10-minute walk from the amphitheatre.

Our tip: The easiest trip: buses run often until the evening, so you can stay for sunset.

The city of a thousand windows

Berat

A UNESCO World Heritage town of white Ottoman houses stacked up the hillside, with a castle where people still live inside the walls.

Travel time
About 2 hours
Ticket
500–700 lek
Buses
Every 30–40 minutes, about 06:00–17:00

What to see

  • Mangalem and GoricaThe two old quarters facing each other across the river Osum.
  • Berat CastleA living citadel with churches, a mosque ruin and wide views.
  • Onufri MuseumIcons by Albania's great 16th-century painter, inside the castle.

When you arrive: The Berat bus station is outside the centre; take a local bus or a short taxi to the old town.

Our tip: Leave early: the last buses back to Tirana go around 16:00. Or stay one night, it is worth it.

Castle, lake and mountain views

Shkodër

The north's cultural capital, between Lake Shkodër and the Albanian Alps, with the legendary Rozafa Castle above town.

Travel time
About 2 hours (up to 3 in summer traffic)
Ticket
About 500 lek
Buses
Every 30–60 minutes, 07:00–18:00

What to see

  • Rozafa CastleThe hilltop fortress of the Rozafa legend, with views over three rivers and the lake.
  • PedonaljaThe colourful pedestrian street of cafés and old merchant houses.
  • Marubi MuseumAlbania's national photography museum, from the 1850s on.

When you arrive: Drivers usually stop near Democracy Square (Sheshi Demokracia), in the centre.

Our tip: A long day trip. Rent a bike in town for the flat ride to the lake, and check the time of the last bus back.
