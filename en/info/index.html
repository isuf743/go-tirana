City buses

A ride on a city line costs 40 lek. A few lines that go out to the suburbs cost a little more.

You pay in cash on board: a conductor comes to you after you get on. There is no ticket machine and no travel card for visitors, so keep coins and small notes.

Buses run from about 6:00 to 23:00. Use our planner to see which line goes where you want.

From the airport to the centre

The Rinas Express bus runs between Tirana International Airport and the city centre once an hour, every day. The ticket costs 400 lek, paid on board.

In the city it stops behind the Palace of Culture, on Skanderbeg Square. The ride takes about 35 to 45 minutes, depending on traffic.

The official airport taxis wait at the marked rank outside arrivals. The fare to the centre is fixed at around €20 to €25, paid in cash. Ignore people inside the terminal offering you a ride.

Taxis

Uber and Bolt do not work in Albania. Local taxi apps do, and they show the price before you ride.

If you take a taxi from the street, agree on the price or check that the meter is on before you set off.

Money

Albania's currency is the lek (ALL), not the euro. Prices you see in shops and on buses are in lek.

Buses and most taxis take cash only, and drivers often cannot change large notes. Carry small notes and coins.

Prices and timetables can change. Check on board or with the operator before you travel. Last checked: November 2026
