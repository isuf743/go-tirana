One day in Tirana

If you only have one day, this is the route we'd take. It stays in the centre, so you can walk the whole way and never need a taxi.

10Stops
3.1 kmOn foot
NoneBus rides
Not on MondaysBest day
  1. 1

    09:00

    QendraTime there: 20–45 min

    Skanderbeg Square

    Start in the middle of everything and walk the square while it's still quiet.

    Walk about 3 min200 m Walking directions
  2. 2

    09:30

    QendraTime there: 15–30 min

    Et'hem Bey Mosque

    Step inside to see the painted walls. Visit outside prayer times and dress modestly.

    Walk about 2 min50 m Walking directions
  3. 3

    09:50

    QendraTime there: 15–30 min

    Clock Tower

    Right next door. If it's open, climb the narrow stairs for a view over the square.

    Walk about 2 min100 m Walking directions
  4. 4

    10:30

    QendraTime there: 1–1.5 h

    Bunk'Art 2

    An hour underground to understand the communist years before you see the rest of the city.

    Walk about 9 min700 m Walking directions
  5. 5

    12:30

    Pazari i RiTime there: 45 min – 2 h

    New Bazaar

    Wander the market hall: cheese, honey, olives and homemade raki.

    Walk about 2 min150 m Walking directions
  6. 6

    13:00

    Pazari i RiTime there: 1–2 h

    Oda Garden

    Lunch. Ask for a mix of traditional starters to share.

    Walk about 12 min950 m Walking directions
  7. 7

    15:00

    QendraTime there: 20–40 min

    Pyramid of Tirana

    Climb the steps to the top for a free view down the boulevard.

    Walk about 5 min350 m Walking directions
  8. 8

    16:00

    BllokuTime there: 10–20 min

    Postbllok Memorial

    A bunker, a piece of the Berlin Wall and pillars from a prison camp, at the gate of Blloku.

    Walk about 6 min450 m Walking directions
  9. 9

    16:30

    BllokuTime there: 2–5 h

    Blloku

    Coffee and a slow walk through the district that was once closed to ordinary Albanians.

    Walk about 4 min250 m Walking directions
  10. 10

    18:30

    BllokuTime there: 1–2.5 h

    Sky Tower

    End with a drink at the revolving bar as the lights come on. Closed on Mondays: then finish on top of the Pyramid instead.

    Hours: Tue–Sun 17:30–00:00

Times are a suggestion, so move them around as you like. Opening hours can change: check them before you go.
