A rainy day

When the weather turns, stay indoors and go deep into Albania's history. The longest walk between stops is under ten minutes.

    The secret police's surveillance centre, with the microphones and files they used.

    Hours: Daily 09:00–19:00

    15:30

    QendraTime there: 45 min – 1.5 h

    National Gallery of Arts

    Albanian art from icons to socialist realism. If the rain stops, see the statue garden at the back.

    Walk about 6 min400 m Walking directions
