Nightlife

Cinco Cavalli

Cinco Cavalli Premium Club

A big show club with concerts, stage acts and table service.

About

Cinco Cavalli, on Rruga Selim Brahja in Sauk, is one of Tirana's largest premium clubs. Nights are built as shows: live performances by Albanian artists, dancers and DJs, with most guests at reserved tables.

Good to know

  • Reserve a table in advance, especially for concert nights.
  • It is outside the centre: take a taxi back at night.

Bars and clubs change often. Check current opening hours before you go.
