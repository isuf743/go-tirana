Dajti Ekspres Cable Car

Teleferiku Dajti Ekspres

A cable car ride from the edge of the city up Mount Dajti.

About

In around fifteen minutes, the gondolas climb over villages and forest to the slopes of Mount Dajti, with Tirana spreading out below. At the top you'll find restaurants, walking trails and some of the best views of the city.

Good to know

  • It may stop in strong wind or storms; check the weather before you go.
  • Bunk'Art 1 is a short walk from the lower station.
