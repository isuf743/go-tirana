Et'hem Bey Mosque

Xhamia e Et'hem Beut

A small Ottoman-era mosque with rare painted landscapes on its walls.

About

Completed in the early 19th century, the mosque is famous for its frescoes of trees, waterfalls and bridges, motifs seldom seen in Islamic art. Closed during the communist ban on religion, it reopened in 1991 when thousands gathered here, a moment many Albanians still remember.

Good to know

  • Visitors are welcome outside prayer times; dress modestly and remove your shoes.
  • Pair it with the Clock Tower right next door.
