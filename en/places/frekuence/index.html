Nightlife

Frekuence

An underground house and techno club by the Artificial Lake.

About

Below the lighthouse of the Fari complex, near Tirana's Artificial Lake, Frekuence is the city's address for electronic music. Expect house and techno from local and international artists, a dark room and a crowd that comes to dance.

Good to know

  • Nights start around 23:00, usually Friday and Saturday.
  • Check the programme and buy tickets online before you go.

Bars and clubs change often. Check current opening hours before you go.
