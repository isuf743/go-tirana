House of Leaves

Shtëpia me Gjethe

The museum of secret surveillance, in the building where it actually happened.

About

Named after the ivy that covers its walls, this villa housed the Sigurimi's surveillance centre. Opened as a museum in 2017, it displays bugging devices, hidden cameras and files, and explains how ordinary citizens were watched for decades.

Good to know

  • Rich in text and audio, so allow time to read.
  • Good on a rainy day, a few minutes from the square.
