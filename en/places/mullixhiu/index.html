Food & markets

Mullixhiu

Chef Bledar Kola's restaurant, reinventing traditional Albanian food with local ingredients.

About

Mullixhiu means "the miller". In a small wood-panelled room at the entrance to the Grand Park, chef Bledar Kola takes classic Albanian recipes and rebuilds them with seasonal produce from local farms. It is one of the best-known places in the city to taste Albanian cooking at its most creative.

Good to know

  • The room is small, so book a table online before you go.
  • Easy to combine with a walk around the Grand Park lake.
