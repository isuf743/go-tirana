History & culture

National Historical Museum

Muzeu Historik Kombëtar

Albania's largest museum, from Illyrian treasures to the communist era.

About

Opened in 1981, the museum is recognisable from afar by the giant mosaic on its façade, known as 'The Albanians'. Inside, the galleries walk you through antiquity, the Middle Ages, the struggle for independence and the dictatorship, including a sober pavilion dedicated to its victims.

Good to know

  • Plan at least 90 minutes; the antiquity halls alone deserve time.
  • Closed on Mondays.
