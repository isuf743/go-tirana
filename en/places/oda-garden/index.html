Food & markets

Oda Garden

Traditional Albanian home cooking in an old house and garden next to the New Bazaar.

About

Oda is the guest room of a traditional Albanian house, and here you can eat at low tables in a rebuilt one or outside in a large enclosed garden. The menu covers home-style dishes from around the country, such as tavë kosi and lakror, along with house raki.

Good to know

  • Ask the staff to suggest a mix of starters to share.
  • Two minutes' walk from the New Bazaar, so visit both together.
