Nightlife

Puls House Club

Blloku's house and afro-house club, with international DJs.

About

Puls is the place for house music in Blloku: a strong sound system, lighting built for the dance floor and a programme of local and international DJs, often playing afro-house until late.

Good to know

  • The best nights are Friday and Saturday after midnight.
  • Magic Club is right next door, for a more commercial, pop and Balkan night.
  • Club nights change: check the venue's social media for tonight's line-up and entry.

Bars and clubs change often. Check current opening hours before you go.
