QTU (Universe Shopping Centre)

QTU – Qendra Tregtare Univers

One of Tirana's oldest big malls, on the Durrës highway, with a hypermarket and a cinema.

About

Open since 2005 at kilometre 6 of the Tirana–Durrës highway, QTU is where many locals do their big weekly shop. Besides the large Carrefour hypermarket there are fashion and electronics stores, a food court and a Cineplexx cinema. It is a practical stop if you are heading to or from Durrës or the airport side of the city.

Good to know

  • City bus L4 (Centre – Vorë) has a stop called QTU right by the entrance.
  • Cross the highway only at the footbridge or traffic lights.
