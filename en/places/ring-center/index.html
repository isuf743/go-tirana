Shopping malls

Ring Center

A mid-sized mall just west of the centre, with three floors of shops, restaurants and a kids' area.

About

Ring Center sits on the city's ring road, within walking distance of the centre, and gathers around 60 shops on three levels: clothing and shoe chains, cosmetics, electronics and home goods, plus places to eat. It is less hectic than the big malls on the edge of town and easy to reach on foot or by the green circular line.

Good to know

  • The green circular line L16 stops at Karl Topia, a minute away.
  • About 20 minutes on foot from Skanderbeg Square.
