Shopping malls

TEG – Tirana East Gate

Albania's biggest mall: around 180 shops, a cinema and a big food court, on the road to Elbasan.

About

Opened in 2011 about 4 km south-east of the centre, TEG spreads over two long floors with international fashion chains, electronics, a hypermarket, a Cineplexx multiplex and plenty of cafés and restaurants. Right next to it is the Eastern Bus Terminal, where coaches leave for the south-east of the country and abroad, so it is also a handy stop before or after a trip.

Good to know

  • City buses L2 and L8/A run from the centre to the mall; allow about an hour door to door, or 15–20 minutes by taxi.
  • Weekends get crowded in the afternoon; weekday mornings are calmest.
