History & culture

Tirana Mosaic

Mozaiku i Tiranës

Late Roman floor mosaics, uncovered in the middle of a residential area.

About

Discovered by chance in 1972, the site preserves mosaics from a Roman villa that was later turned into an early Christian basilica. It is one of the few visible traces of Tirana long before it became a city.

Good to know

  • Small and quiet: best for history lovers.
  • Combine it with a walk along Rruga e Durrësit.
