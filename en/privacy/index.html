Privacy policy

Who we are

Go Tirana is an independent guide to Tirana run by DankeFlow Agency. For any question about your data, write to info@dankeflow.com or use the contact form.

What we collect

We only collect what you send us through the contact form: your name, email address, topic and message. We store it on our server to answer you and keep it for up to 12 months, then delete it. We don't sell or share it.

Email list

If you join our email list, we store your email address, the language you chose and the date you signed up. We use it only to send occasional news about the guide, and never share or sell it. To be removed, write to info@dankeflow.com and we will delete it.

Statistics

If you allow it in the privacy banner, we count visits with GoatCounter. It records the page, the referring site, browser type and country, without cookies and without storing your IP address. You can change your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Your browser's storage

We save your language, your privacy choice and, in the bus planner, your calibration measurements in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time.

Third-party services

To show maps and photos, your browser connects directly to: Google Maps (all maps on the site, including the bus planner, under Google's privacy policy) and Wikimedia Commons (photos). These providers receive your IP address as part of the connection, under their own privacy policies. Fonts are served from our own server.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. The contact form is protected against spam without tracking you: we use a hidden field, a time check and a limit on how many messages can be sent from one connection.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to its use, by writing to info@dankeflow.com. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country; in Albania this is the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDP).
